What is DrinkPrime?

DrinkPrime is a Bengaluru-based company that rents out water purifiers on a monthly subscription instead of selling them. You don't buy the machine. You pay a monthly fee, and installation, servicing and filter changes are handled by the company.

The purifiers are internet-connected, so the company can track the device's health and schedule service visits. Models offered include RO, RO+UV, copper, alkaline and under-the-sink options, depending on your city and water source.
